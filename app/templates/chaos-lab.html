Chaos Engineering Lab

Controlled failure injection for live demo. All scenarios auto-reset via Redis TTL. Disable in production. Requires FEATURE_CHAOS_API_ENABLED=true.

Circuit breaker

Forces the YouTube API circuit breaker open. All extraction requests fail immediately with a 503. The circuit breaker auto-recovers via half-open to closed transition.

Worker crash

Simulates a worker crash mid-processing. The job stays in "processing" state until the zombie sweeper detects and reclaims it, demonstrating automatic recovery.

Database failover

Raises a transient OperationalError after the worker claims a job. The retry chain fires with exponential backoff and jitter, and the job is rescheduled via the outbox pattern.

429 spike

Sets the throttle spike Redis flag consumed by the AI throttle predictor. The predictor detects the spike and raises the risk score above 0.7. Requires the ai-throttle-predictor change.

Bulk submit videos

Submit multiple random videos to the download queue at once. Videos are picked from a pool of 30+ URLs. Jobs are staggered with 150ms delays for a cascading effect.

Worker throttle

Slows down video processing by adding a random 5-20 second delay before each job. Makes the "processing" state visible on the dashboard for longer. Also reduces yt-dlp download speed to simulate slow network conditions.
